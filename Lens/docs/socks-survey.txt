--- PASS 23 ADDENDUM: THE PAIR, AND WHAT WAS BUILT ---------------------------------------------------
THE SHARED-CHART QUESTION. The two socks are SEPARATE meshes (sock-left-b, sock-right-b; 11,408
triangles each, welded from 22,816 to 6,010 vertices apiece) on SEPARATE materials (medias,
medias.001), but their UV charts are one layout shifted 0.11 in u. Rasterised at 1024, 188,278
texels carry triangles from BOTH socks: 59.8% of the left sock's texels and 56.5% of the right's,
18.0% of the atlas. Every cross-island overlap the survey reports is left-versus-right; within one
sock there is none. So one bake sampled by both materials would print every sticker on both socks
(shifted 7 cm); a bake per material prints on one.

DISTORTION. Per-triangle cm/u on the body island: p10 60.5, p50 65.4, p90 73.1; cm/v 49.9 / 61.6
/ 71.2. Within each generated region the unwrap's rotation stays within 7-34 degrees (p10-p90),
against 2-10 on the cap. The centroid triangle of the body island reads 9.5 cm/v against an island
mean of 61 — which is why this garment uses an area-weighted mean Jacobian (survey.py jacobian=
"mean"), splits along the axis its normals actually sweep across (splitAxis="sweep", 25 degree
regions) and searches a slice for its best square centre when the centroid falls in the ankle notch.

VERDICT: PASSED as ONE garment with TWO bake targets. 42 regions (21 per sock), shin halves 9.0 x
23.2 cm at max scale 2.0, sticker side 4.5 cm. The chart's seam runs down the front of each shin,
so a sticker lives on the left or the right half of a shin, never across its centre line.
Textures: two 1024 albedos (flat grey with a brand doodle, unused: fabricFromAlbedo false) shrunk
to 64 x 64.
