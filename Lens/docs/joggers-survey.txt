Source: ~/Downloads/jogging.glb (Blender "Sweatpants Mockup", 39.6 MB). Lens Studio's glTF loader
refused the file as shipped ("Invalid image data for image[0] sweatpants_normal": an 8000 x 8000
JPEG), so it had never reached Assets. tools/glbreduce.py rewrote it — normal maps to 1024, the
unused "Example" albedo to 64, the mockup's two studio-light meshes dropped — and that 10.3 MB
file imported cleanly as Assets/joggers/jogging_reduced.

GEOMETRY. The pants mesh is ONE LEG under a full waistband ring. Of 169,849 triangles below
the waist, 168,097 are left of the waist centre and NONE lie past x = +0.05 — the right leg is
absent (the mockup's mirror was never applied on export). The cloth is double-walled: every
face has an opposite-facing twin 0.2 mm behind it, which is why the island's summed UV area
reads 307% of the atlas and why every texel carries both a front- and a back-facing triangle.
232,680 triangles in all (the cap is 14,690; the hoodie that "behaved badly" was 26,628).

ATLAS. Injective apart from the double wall: legs A/B in the chart are the front and back halves
of the single leg tube, with at most one cloth sheet under a texel (2,619 of 3,072 sampled
bins), no +X/-X mirroring, and one real cross-island overlap — the hem strip (island 1) shares
4.4% of the atlas with the body (island 0).

to 31 cm tall. A 5 cm sticker does not fit any panel the survey can inscribe (max scale 0.64 on
the body island, 0.05 on the hem, nothing on the straps).
